Research Contributions: Overcoming Base Paper Limitations

Key architectural advancements implemented for Prof. Prathap Kumar M K's evaluation

Limitation #1 in Marzuki et al.

Static Demographic Features Only (No Temporal/Seasonal Awareness)

Tourism destination suitability shifts dramatically between seasons. A hill station or beach has completely different visitor experiences in Monsoon vs. Winter.

Our Solution: Feature-engineered Indian seasonal windows into training inputs & re-ranking multipliers.
Limitation #2 in Marzuki et al.

Single-Class Classification (No Ranked Top-N List)

Base paper only outputs one broad category class rather than a practical, user-facing ranked recommendation list with match confidence percentages.

Our Solution: Extended the Stacking ensemble to output Top-N ranked destinations with composite scoring (60%–98.5%).
Limitation #3 in Marzuki et al.

Ignored Financial Constraints & Group Types

Two users with the same age/gender have different travel behaviors depending on budget levels and whether traveling solo, as a couple, or with family.

Our Solution: Synthesized budget tiers (`Low, Medium, Luxury`) and companion dynamics into the feature pipeline.
